Two Levels of Coverage, Stated Honestly

Full Service — San Diego & Riverside Counties

These are the counties I work in person. In-person interior and exterior inspections, drive-by assignments, and the complete range of report types, including the Standard appraisal that the IRS requires for improved residential property.

Desktop Appraisals — All 58 California Counties

Statewide, I prepare USPAP-compliant desktop and retrospective appraisals without a property visit, working from public records, MLS data and client-supplied photographs. The report discloses that scope plainly, which is why attorneys and courts accept it for the purposes it fits.

I will tell you which product your situation actually requires — including when it is the more expensive one. A desktop report does not satisfy IRS requirements for improved residential property.
