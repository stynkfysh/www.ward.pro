Antioch Property Appraisal — Contra Costa County, CA

Antioch began as a river town on the San Joaquin and spent a century as a coal-and-paper mill community before the subdivisions of the 1990s and 2000s pushed it south toward the Black Diamond hills. Two cities now share one jurisdiction: a small historic Rivertown core, and a very large expanse of newer tract above it. Antioch matters are desktop assignments for me, built from county records, MLS history and whatever photographs the client can gather.

How Property Trades in Antioch

Antioch prices off commute cost more than off anything intrinsic to its housing, which makes the city's value pattern unusually sensitive to the direction of rates and fuel. Rivertown and the older north-side blocks hold pre-war cottages and 1940s mill housing on small lots, condition running from deferred to fully renovated. The 1970s and 1980s tracts in the middle of the city — Sycamore, Country Hills, Williamson Ranch — are conventional stucco subdivisions of consistent size. South of Lone Tree Way, the Dallas Ranch, Deer Valley and Black Diamond-edge subdivisions built from the late 1990s onward bring larger two-story plans, three-car garages, community facilities district assessments and active HOAs that the older areas do not carry. That south-of-Lone-Tree line is the most important boundary in the city for comparable selection. The eBART extension to the Hillcrest station changed accessibility for the east side, and proximity to it reads in the data. Investor ownership is a meaningful share of the older stock, so tenanted condition and deferred maintenance appear in the record more often than in neighboring Brentwood or Oakley.

What Anchors the Area

Black Diamond Mines Regional Preserve covers the coal-country hills behind the city, and Contra Loma Regional Park sits between them and the subdivisions. The Antioch Marina and the Antioch Bridge connect the waterfront to Highway 160 and the Delta. Antioch and Pittsburg Center eBART stations feed the BART system at Pittsburg/Bay Point. Antioch Unified operates Deer Valley and Antioch High Schools; Kaiser Permanente Antioch is the principal medical anchor. Highway 4 was widened through the city in stages, which reshaped the commute.

Valuation Issues Specific to Antioch

Desktop work in Antioch turns on getting the vintage and the district right. I verify whether a property sits inside a community facilities district, because special assessments change the payment a buyer underwrites, and I confirm HOA status in the newer subdivisions. Two-story plans from the 2000s build-out are not substitutes for 1970s single-story ranches a mile north, so the comparable set stays inside the same era and street pattern. Mapped flood exposure near the river, expansive-clay soils in parts of the south end, and solar leases on newer roofs all get documented. For retrospective valuations I work from the archived listing photographs and the permit file rather than a visit.
