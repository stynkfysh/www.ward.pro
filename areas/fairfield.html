Fairfield Property Appraisal — Solano County, CA

Halfway between the Bay Area and the capital, Fairfield has lived off three things for a century: Travis Air Force Base, the farms and vineyards of Suisun Valley, and the Interstate 80 commute. Its housing runs from 1950s base-era tract on the flats to hillside custom homes above Green Valley. I take Fairfield assignments as desktop valuations, built from county records, MLS history and client-provided photographs.

How Property Trades in Fairfield

The west-side and central split in Fairfield is not a postal artifact — it tracks a real difference in product and in buyer. West of Interstate 680, Green Valley, Rancho Solano, Cordelia and Southbridge hold newer and larger homes, golf-course frontage, hillside lots with Suisun Bay and valley exposure, and HOA and community facilities district structures. Central and north Fairfield around the downtown grid, North Texas Street and West Texas Street hold the older base-era and 1960s tract, smaller and more condition-variable, with the city's apartment concentration nearby. Paradise Valley and the blocks around the municipal golf course sit between the two in both age and price. Suisun Valley along the north edge is agricultural-residential: vineyard parcels, wells, septic systems and outbuildings that require a different comparable pool entirely. Travis Air Force Base generates a steady relocation market and a rental demand floor that civilian commuter cities of the same size lack. Lagoon Valley and the Jepson Parkway corridor are the active growth edges, and new construction there carries assessment obligations the older city does not.

What Anchors the Area

Travis Air Force Base sits immediately east and is the county's largest employer. The Suisun Valley wine region and the Rockville Hills Regional Park occupy the hills to the north and west. Solano Town Center and the Jelly Belly factory draw regional traffic, and NorthBay Medical Center anchors healthcare. Fairfield-Suisun Unified serves the city. Interstate 80 meets Interstate 680 here, and the Fairfield-Vacaville Hannigan station put Capitol Corridor rail service in the corridor. Pena Adobe and Lagoon Valley Park sit along the Vacaville boundary.

Valuation Issues Specific to Fairfield

Fairfield's two halves do not substitute for one another, so the first thing I establish from the desk is which market the subject belongs to. Green Valley and Rancho Solano properties need view, slope and usable site area isolated, plus HOA and special-assessment detail. Downtown and north-side subjects need condition read carefully out of listing photographs and permit history, because the age band is uniform there and condition carries the entire spread. Suisun Valley parcels are their own problem: agricultural zoning, Williamson Act contracts, well and septic systems and planted acreage all have to be addressed, and the comparable pool extends well past the city limit.
