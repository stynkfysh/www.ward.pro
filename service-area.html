Service Area

126 communities across 17 counties have a page here. Desktop appraisal coverage reaches all 58 California counties.

My coverage comes in two levels, and I would rather be precise about the difference than imply I can inspect a house in Modesto on Tuesday.

Full Service — San Diego & Riverside Counties

I work these two counties in person. Standard appraisals with interior and exterior inspection, drive-by assignments, two-to-four unit inspections, and the complete product range are all available, including the Standard report the IRS requires for improved residential property.

Desktop & Retrospective — Statewide

Anywhere else in California, I prepare USPAP-compliant desktop appraisals without a property visit, built from public records, MLS data and photographs you provide. The report discloses that scope. This is the normal instrument for an out-of-area estate, trust or date-of-death assignment.

The counties below are the ones with dedicated pages on this site. If your property is in a California county not listed, desktop coverage still applies — send me the address and I will confirm.
